# ATS and resume validation

- Generate content only from verified profile facts; never invent experience or skills.
- Validate the final PDF/DOCX artifact, not only its source model.
- Separate technical extractability from job relevance.
- Produce evidence for findings and version reports against exact resume/job/profile versions.
- Never claim compatibility with every ATS or present an internal score as a hiring prediction.
